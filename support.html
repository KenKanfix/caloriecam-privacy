Frequently Asked Questions

  • Where is my data stored? Your meal photos and food logs are stored only on your device using local storage. They are not collected or stored on any server by us.
  • Where do the nutrition numbers come from? When you snap or upload a food photo, it is sent to the Google Gemini AI service to estimate calories and macronutrients. Results should be treated as estimates, not medical advice.
  • How do I delete my data? Delete any logged meal from within the app, or uninstall the app to erase everything.
